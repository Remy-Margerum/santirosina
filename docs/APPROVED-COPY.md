## Santi Rosina – An Introduction
*Text to be used on the website and in marketing materials*

From one of the most beautiful vineyard estates in California comes a collection of limited-production wines from
Happy Canyon of Santa Barbara. Crafted by acclaimed Santa Barbara winemaker Douglas Margerum, these wines reflect
remarkable terroir, patient farming, and restrained, site-driven winemaking. *[home, "An Introduction"]*

Set against the rolling hills of eastern Santa Barbara County, the estate vineyard is planted with Italian and
Bordeaux varietals ideally suited to the warm days and cool evenings of Happy Canyon. The dramatic landscape,
mineral-rich soils, and extended growing season produce wines of notable depth, structure, and elegance.
*[home, "An Introduction"]*

The collection includes: *[home, "The Collection"; the shop's wine headings; each wine page's lead]*

- Sauvignon Blanc — vibrant and textured, balancing citrus, tropical fruit, minerality, and freshness with a style
  reminiscent of the great white wines of Bordeaux and Friuli.
- Cabernet Sauvignon — powerful yet refined, with layered dark fruit, graphite, and polished tannins that speak to the
  pedigree of the vineyard site.
- Nebbiolo — one of California's rarest and most compelling expressions of the varietal, showing rose petals, wild
  strawberry, tar, and savory earth in a style inspired by the Langhe hills of Piedmont.
- Sangiovese — bright, energetic, and food-driven, offering notes of cherry, dried herbs, and Tuscan-like vibrancy with
  exceptional balance and age-worthiness.

Produced in small quantities and available through our wine club and select restaurant partners, these wines are made
to be shared around a table, aged in a cellar, and revisited across vintages. *[home, "The Wine Club"; the wine club
page's intro]* Crafted not for trends, but for the table, the cellar, and the long view of California wine. *[the
shop's intro]*

## Our Story *[the Our Story page, in order]*

Every great wine has a place it comes from, and a place it's meant to take you.

Santi Rosina was created from the belief that the best things in life are cultivated slowly. Meals that linger around
the table. Vineyards tended by hand. Recipes refined over generations. A deep love for the land and for the people
gathered around it.

When we discovered this hidden valley in Happy Canyon of Santa Barbara, it felt less like somewhere new and more like
somewhere familiar, a landscape that could have been lifted from the Italian countryside. The name Santi Rosina honors
that connection: a family's Italian roots, carried across generations and planted in California soil.

Here, Italian and Bordeaux varietals grow side by side. Sangiovese and Nebbiolo share the vineyard with Cabernet
Sauvignon and Merlot, bringing together two great wine traditions in one remarkable estate. A meeting of Old World
patience and California possibility, where heritage and innovation exist in harmony.

Olive trees, orchards, fields of lavender and sunflowers, and open pasture share the landscape, creating an estate
reminiscent of Tuscany. The same soil that nourishes the vines supports everything grown here, reflecting a philosophy
that great wine belongs next to great food and good living.

Every decision is intentional. Every vine is tended by hand. Every bottle is crafted with patience rather than haste.
Not because it is the easiest path, but because it is the one that produces wines with authenticity, depth, and a
sense of place.

Santi Rosina is an expression of two worlds brought together on a single hillside. Italian soul. California terroir.
Wines created to be shared around a table where conversation lasts into the evening and every glass invites another
story.

## Mission Statement *[home and Our Story, "Our Mission"]*

At Santi Rosina, we believe great wine is rooted in heritage, shaped by place, and meant to bring people together. From
our estate in Happy Canyon of Santa Barbara, we grow Italian and Bordeaux varietals, honoring generations of family
tradition while embracing the character of California. Every bottle reflects our commitment to craftsmanship,
authenticity, and creating wines that inspire connection around the table.
